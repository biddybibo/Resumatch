Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Resumatch (resumatch.help)

Overview

Resumatch ("we," "us," or "our") is an AI-powered resume tailoring tool. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights around that data.

What We Collect

When you use Resumatch, we may collect:

  • Your email address (via Google sign-in)
  • Resume content you upload or paste into the app
  • Job descriptions you provide for tailoring
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used) for improving the product

We do not collect payment information directly — payments are handled by Stripe.

How We Use Your Data

  • To generate tailored resumes using AI
  • To authenticate your account and manage your session
  • To improve the product based on usage patterns
  • To send transactional emails (e.g. account confirmation)

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your resume content for advertising.

Third-Party Services

Resumatch uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — authentication and database
  • Anthropic / OpenAI — AI processing of resume content
  • Stripe — subscription billing
  • Vercel — hosting and infrastructure

Each of these services has their own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Resume content is processed in real time and is not stored permanently unless you explicitly save it within the app.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of any non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at admin@resumatch.help.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication via Supabase, and environment-variable-protected API keys. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach out at admin@resumatch.help.
